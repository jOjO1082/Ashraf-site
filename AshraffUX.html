About

Creativity you can use

I love creativity, usable design and design communication. Across communications, media production, design and motion graphics, I have helped organisations improve their image and reputation with content that works.

I plan and deliver with my team in line with global practice, take direction well and lead juniors toward shared goals. Working with academic institutions and industry has taught me to adapt to different work cultures and to stay gender sensitive. I also run my own media production practice for clients and multimedia enthusiasts.

With friends, I started Heal TV, an ethical online television channel that creates alternative programming with international-appeal production to encourage responsibility. I supply the motion graphics and have been the video editor since day one.
